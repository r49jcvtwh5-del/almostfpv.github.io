About Me

Federico Ezequiel Darraidou

Based in Porto, Portugal, for the past 7 years, I specialize in capturing aerial footage at the highest quality available on the market today. I cover music events, exhibitions, trade shows, and all types of events, both indoors and outdoors.

My approach combines precise piloting technique with cinematic vision to deliver unique shots that elevate any production. I work with state-of-the-art professional equipment capable of recording in 4K at 120fps with D-Log 10-bit color profile.

Certifications & Experience

I hold all necessary permits to operate professionally throughout the European Union:

Services

Professional aerial imaging for all types of projects

Music Events

Dynamic aerial coverage of concerts, festivals, and live events. Capture the energy of the crowd and stage design from impossible perspectives with agile, safe FPV drones.

Exhibitions & Shows

Aerial documentation of galleries, fairs, and exhibitions. Precise indoor flights with propeller-protected drones to capture art and architecture from unique angles.

Corporate Events

Professional coverage of conferences, product launches, and corporate events. High-quality footage for marketing and social media.

Vehicle Chasing

High-speed pursuit of cars, motorcycles, and boats. Ideal for commercials, automotive content, and sports productions.

Private Events

Weddings, celebrations, and private events with discreet and elegant coverage. Capture special moments from cinematic perspectives.

Cinematic Production

Aerial shots for film, advertising, and digital content. Professional 4K 120fps quality with D-Log profile for maximum post-production flexibility.
